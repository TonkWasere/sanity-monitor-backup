## Ticketopia Event

![screenshot]({STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/41581512/a9fbab5406ab5493254a1eab1edbd05220fd90fa.png)

In autumn, not only leaves fall from the trees, but also Act Tickets, which can be obtained for PvE challenges or for challenges in the Coliseum. The event will run from October 14 to 28.

There will be 16 challenges in the event, 8 for each format. You can complete them in any combination: either 8 in PvE, or 8 in the Coliseum, or 4 and 4.

#### Locations to complete PvE challenges

They are only available in the North, Wild North and Lyubech-3. Challenges will not be completed in the peaceful South. Also, the reward does not depend on the selected location.

#### What challenges will there be

- PvE: only open-world activities not tied to direct competition with other players. Outposts, airdrops, and installations in the Wild North do not count.
- Coliseum: destroying enemy squads, capturing installations, activating rifts, and other tasks.

The remaining challenges will be announced when the event begins.

#### Rewards

For each completed challenge, a small reward is given. Larger rewards are granted for reaching certain milestones of completed challenges:

- 2 challenges: 100 Act Tickets
- 4 challenges: 150 Act Tickets
- 6 challenges: Veil style for Albatross Interceptor.

![screenshot]({STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/41581512/ed413b6d3ad14b1b6d1736cec72067dc8d891111.png)

- 8 challenges: 250 Act Tickets

To claim the main prizes, you only need to complete any 8 out of 16 challenges, freely mixing both formats.

## Economy, rewards and progression

We've reworked the reward system and economy to make progression across locations more tangible and strengthen the position of the Faction North. Certain activities have also received a significant reward buff.

**Now you're farming more than before. **Here's why:

Previously, inventories got clogged with junk — small items took up space and were worth almost nothing.

According to the analytics data, players did not claim nearly half of the event rewards due to their low value and excess inventory space.

We've reworked rewards on the Faction North, aligning them ideologically with Wild North rewards. Fillers now take up less space and are worth more — the value of a player's inventory relative to its weight has increased.

**Let's show the numbers using Mines as an example: **

Let’s say you came to a location and cleared 100 events: previously, just under half of the items awarded as rewards for clearing events were of extremely low value. As a result, receiving them was often seen more as a burden than a useful reward: players frequently sent such items to the Trash to avoid taking up inventory space.

With the update to the reward pool, the value of the items awarded will increase, making it worthwhile for players to collect each one.

The tables below show the results of our calculations for two scenarios: under current player behavior, where low-value items are not collected, and after the reward system is changed, when players will collect all awarded items.

**Mutants** — previously loot occupied 66 inventory slots and was worth 152 thousand. Now it takes 49 slots and is worth 351 thousand. Less space, more money.

## Faction North

![screenshot]({STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/41581512/b82d029a4aa5ff73c512f6ea03356b56132b9a3c.png)

### Events

Let’s start with the main thing. Right now, the rewards for clearing events in the Faction North don’t feel valuable enough. Some of the items seem more like junk than a reward for completing an activity in a more dangerous area compared to the South.

To straighten things out, we’ll add new items for sale, while some existing items will receive more convenient characteristics: larger stack sizes, lower weight, and higher value.

Thanks to this, your inventory will fill up more slowly, encumbrance will occur later, and it will become more profitable to take all the items after clearing events.

And, of course, extracting with a full backpack of loot will become more profitable as well!

#### Outposts

![screenshot]({STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/41581512/872ff3169ef1e974936210e967fc4594d38691a9.png)

Separately, we’d like to note that we will be changing Outposts rewards. Items sold to Arsenal will become more convenient, but the amount of Arsenal reputation that can be framed will remain at roughly the same level. **

#### Abandoned Installation, Arthunters, and Scientists

![screenshot]({STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/41581512/009054f265c18c36bd4139e4ef33a3c453c223c8.png)

The contents of the Abandoned Installation, Arthunters, and Scientists events will also be reworked so that their rewards better correspond to the type of activity.

### Caches

![screenshot]({STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/41581512/498e4f827a2f26456dbcea43f393962fdff2887a.png)

Right now caches are an extremely unprofitable and even dangerous activity that forces you to put your weapon away.

Therefore, we will significantly increase the rewards from caches in the Faction North and revise the items that can be obtained from them. They will take up fewer inventory slots and weigh less.

### Jackpots

We’ll also introduce a “jackpot” mechanic to make caches and NPC events feel less like chores and more like activities that can pleasantly surprise you.

The logic is simple: the more dangerous the location, the higher the chance of getting a jackpot. This will apply especially to the gold NPC events.

In the Wild North, loot will receive minor updates as part of the overall system so that rewards remain consistent with the progression: South → Faction North → Wild North.

## Protoartifacts

![screenshot]({STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/41581512/2ae7abe736a7227743eb525456f3edda0c5a9b1c.png)

Does anyone remember where those several legendary protoartifacts in their stash came from? Neither do we, because right now there is almost no location-based progression when farming protoartifacts: they can be obtained in the South at roughly the same rate as in the North.

### Proto-Discharge Cluster and Anomalous Rift

![screenshot]({STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/41581512/9edbef78bcac23f9be95e5c5ac30b284054d7604.png)

Because of this, the northern Proto-Discharge Cluster and Anomalous Rift events remain unpopular, while exclusive and legendary quality protoartifacts have almost disappeared from the economy.

We will introduce location-based progression for farming protoartifacts. Northern territories will become more relevant as a source of rare protoartifacts, but at the same time we will carefully monitor their value so as not to devalue the existing economy.

### Gear Repairs

![screenshot]({STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/41581512/2697891a0b7bea6e2ad544bab7438fb33475c928.png)

As the icing on the cake, we’re also planning to take a separate look at repair costs. And this isn’t about removing money from the economy.

Right now, repairs don’t always seem logical: an armor of a higher rank can sometimes be cheaper to repair, gear of the same tier can have significantly different repair costs, and weapons of the same tier can have different operating costs depending on their caliber.

We’ll review the cost and durability of gear and create a clearer repair calculation model that takes into account the gear type, tier, caliber, and other parameters.

That’s all for now. If anything changes or new details emerge, we’ll be sure to keep you informed, so stay tuned for the news!
